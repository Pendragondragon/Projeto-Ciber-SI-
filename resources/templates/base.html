SAFE-DEPOSIT-BOX

Web application for storing messages in encrypted safes using modern mechanisms of confidentiality, integrity, and authentication.

Quick Flow

1. Deposit a message.

2. Keep the returned secret.

3. Open the safe with the ID and the secret.

Security Features

Protection Methods

Random symmetric key, password derived with a secure algorithm, or RSA asymmetric encryption.

Symmetric Ciphers

AES-256-CBC, AES-256-CTR and ChaCha20.

Asymmetric Encryption

RSA for generating public/private key pairs.

Integrity

HMAC-SHA256 or HMAC-SHA512 over the ciphertext.

Digital Signature

RSA-PSS over the plaintext before encryption.

Automatic Verifications

HMAC validation and digital signature verification upon opening the safe.
